The Sound Behind the Silence

Swami Shailendra Saraswati on Osho, meditation, and what we may have missed.

In Episode 3 of the Osho Sangha Podcast, Swami Shailendra speaks about his journey, the pressures facing young people today, and the living purpose of meditation. The conversation also looks closely at Dynamic and Nadabrahma Meditation and the relationship between inner sound, silence, and witnessing.
