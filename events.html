Featured North Carolina Retreat

Osho Meditation Retreat at Lake Junaluska.

November 12-15, 2026 · Lake Junaluska, North Carolina

The Experience

Meditation and celebration

Four days of Osho active meditations, silent sitting, inquiry, workshops, music, nature walks, and community. Beginners and experienced meditators are welcome.

Daily Rhythm

Thursday through Sunday

Arrival and opening begin Thursday afternoon. Friday and Saturday offer full programs from Dynamic Meditation through the evening gathering. Sunday closes with integration and departures.

Participation

As you like

Contribute any amount that feels right and possible for you. Your contribution supports the four-day retreat program, guided meditations, workshops, evening programs, and facilitator support.

Lodging and meals are not included and are paid directly to Lake Junaluska.

Oshofest New York

October 15-18, 2026 at The Abode.

The schedule is still in flow and will be finalized before the festival, but Dynamic, Kundalini, and Evening Satsang will be consistent features.

Opening

Thursday, October 15, 2026 at 5pm with Osho Kundalini Meditation, followed by dinner and Evening Satsang with the band.

Full Festival Days

Friday and Saturday include full days of meditation with breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Saturday evening includes the beloved Creativity Night.

Closing

Sunday includes breakfast, Sannyas Celebration, and officially ends at 1pm with lunch.

Early Arrival

The Abode opens Wednesday for those who want to arrive early and relax. A nominal Wednesday accommodation fee is charged by The Abode.

Food and Place

The kitchen opens Thursday evening. Before then, restaurants are available nearby in New Lebanon. The Abode is a functioning Sufi community surrounded by nature and walking paths.

Publication Standard

Current details come from organizers and named sources.

Participating organizers can submit directly, while occasional external calendar spotlights are clearly attributed and linked to their original source.

Organizer-confirmed

The host or organizer supplies or confirms the date, location, description, and registration path.

Source-linked

Attributed external listings remain short and send visitors to the organizer's website for final availability, program changes, and registration.

Correctable

Organizers can request an update or removal. Conflicting information is held back until it is resolved.

For Centers

Submit or manage an event.

In the real app, a center manager will log in, add event details, and submit changes for review if needed.

1. Choose host center

Events connect to a center profile, so visitors can see who is hosting and whether the profile is claimed.

2. Add event details

Date, time, format, location, price, registration link, description, photo, facilitator, and contact.

3. Publish

Approved events appear on the global calendar, the host center profile, and relevant location/type searches.
