Donation and Support Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Osho Central support payments are intended to help with platform development, hosting, participating-center profiles, event review, retreat resources, and community writing.

The November 2026 Lake Junaluska retreat is complete and registration is closed. Any contribution offered through the Osho Meditation Retreat USA Wix page is an optional donation supporting future retreat planning and community programs; it is not a registration, lodging, or meal payment.

Support payments are not purchases of Osho books, Osho materials, or official certification. If a payment was made in error, email oshonc369@gmail.com within 14 days and include the payment receipt.

Refunds may depend on the payment processor and the nature of the contribution. Before public launch, every payment link should be tested with a small payment and refund.
