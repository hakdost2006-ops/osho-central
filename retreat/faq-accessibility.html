Frequently Asked Questions

Before you arrive

Do I need previous meditation experience?

No. Beginners and experienced meditators are welcome. Instructions are offered before practices, and questions are encouraged.

How much does the retreat program cost?

There is no fixed program fee. The retreat program contribution is pay as much as you can.

Are lodging and meals included?

No. Rooms and meal plans are reserved and paid separately through Lake Junaluska after you complete retreat registration.

When should I arrive?

Plan to arrive Thursday, November 12 between 2:00 and 5:00 PM. Dinner and the opening evening program follow.

What should I wear?

Bring loose clothing for movement, layers for mountain weather, and walking shoes. Red or maroon clothing may be worn during daytime sessions and white clothing during evening meditation, but neither is required.

Can I participate at my own pace?

Yes. Listen to your body, reduce intensity, rest, or step out when needed. Speak with a facilitator before a session when you have a concern about a practice.

Will there be quiet time?

Yes. The schedule balances active meditation and community time with stillness, rest, nature, and evening quiet hours.

What happens if my plans change?

Email the retreat team about your program registration. Lodging and meal changes follow the separate terms provided by Lake Junaluska.

Accessibility

Please tell us what will help you participate.

Access needs are individual. Contact the retreat team before arrival about mobility, sensory, dietary, communication, rest, or program needs. Contact Lake Junaluska before booking for current details about accessible rooms, parking, routes, bathrooms, and dining facilities.
