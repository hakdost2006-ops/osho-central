Getting There

Lake Junaluska is near Asheville and Waynesville.

Asheville Regional Airport is the closest commercial airport. Charlotte Douglas International Airport is a farther option with a larger route network. Travel times vary with traffic and weather, so confirm your route before departure.

Open Map

Driving approach

From I-40, use Exit 27 toward US 19/23 and then Exit 103 for Lake Junaluska. Check current navigation and road conditions on your travel day.

On-site parking information should be confirmed with the venue when you reserve.

Before You Book

Questions worth confirming with the venue

Room choice

Ask which shared, private, or upgraded rooms are available for your dates and whether a roommate selection is required.

Meal plan

Confirm meal-plan dates, vegetarian options, allergy procedures, and the deadline for dietary requests.

Accessibility

Ask the venue directly about the room, route, bathroom, parking, and dining accommodations you need. Also tell the retreat team so program spaces can be planned.

Venue information can change.Use the Lake Junaluska booking page for current availability, prices, cancellation terms, and accommodation details.

Arrival

A calm first afternoon

  • Aim to arrive Thursday between 2:00 and 5:00 PM.
  • Keep your venue confirmation and retreat contact information available.
  • Bring layers for changing mountain weather and comfortable walking shoes.
  • Allow time to settle before dinner and the opening meditation.
