Speaker and meditation guide

Swami Shailendra Saraswati

Swami Shailendra Saraswati is a medical doctor by training and Osho's younger brother. He has translated and edited Osho's work and has led meditation camps and talks in India and internationally.

At the retreat: Morning discourse and interactive question-and-answer sessions on Friday and Saturday.

Retreat manager and workshop facilitator

Swami Bodhi Soham

Swami Bodhi Soham is a meditation teacher, intuitive coach, and Daoist practitioner. His work draws on Osho meditations, Taoist internal practices, Qigong, Sufi traditions, and embodiment.

Workshop: Sexual Alchemy and Vitality for Men explores breath, awareness, pelvic-floor connection, energetic circulation, vitality, and grounded presence.

Psychotherapist and workshop facilitator

Zumrad Masar

Zumrad Masar is a psychotherapist and experiential group facilitator whose work brings together dreamwork, somatic awareness, meditation, and integration.

Workshop: Dreaming the Self Awake uses guided inquiry, embodiment, and witnessing to meet dreams as living movements of consciousness.
