Why Active Meditation?

Meet the body before asking for silence.

Active meditations use breath, sound, movement, shaking, dance, or expression before a period of stillness. For many people, this sequence makes it easier to notice the body, release held energy, and sit quietly without forcing the mind.

Each person participates at their own pace. These descriptions are an orientation, not a substitute for the instructions given by facilitators.

Care for your bodyModify or pause any movement that does not feel appropriate. Before the session, tell a facilitator about any health consideration that may affect vigorous breathing or movement.

Core Practices

The planned meditation program

Dynamic Meditation

An early-morning sequence of deep breathing, cathartic movement and sound, energetic action, stillness, and celebration.

Kundalini Meditation

A late-afternoon practice moving through shaking, dance, quiet observation, and stillness.

No Mind Meditation

A structured practice using expressive sounds followed by quiet sitting and witnessing.

No-Dimensions Meditation

A centering sequence of coordinated movement and breath, followed by whirling and stillness.

White Robe Meditation

An evening gathering that moves from music and celebration into listening and silence.

Bardo Meditation

A guided practice exploring awareness, impermanence, and conscious transition.

Additional Possibilities

The program can include more than one doorway.

Sound and humming

Nadabrahma and sound-based practices use vibration, gentle movement, and silence to support inward attention.

Dance and celebration

Nataraj, music, and free movement invite participation without choreography or performance.

Silent sitting

Periods of quiet witnessing, rest, and nature allow the effects of active practice to settle.

The final selection may change with the retreat flow. The published schedule shows the current organizing plan.

Preparing

Simple preparation is enough.

  • Wear loose clothing that allows comfortable movement.
  • Avoid a heavy meal immediately before an active session.
  • Bring water and any personal support you use for sitting.
  • Arrive a few minutes early for instructions.
  • Ask questions before the music begins.
